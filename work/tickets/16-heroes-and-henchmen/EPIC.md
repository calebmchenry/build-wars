# Heroes and Henchmen

## Goal

Catalog party NPCs well enough to support hero/team builds, portraits, party composition, and AI notes.

## Scope

* Heroes with names, professions, campaigns, portraits/icons, and unlock/source metadata if useful.
* Hero profession-change rules.
* Hero skill/equipment constraints that differ from players.
* Hero AI notes for skills that behave differently under AI.
* Henchmen metadata if the party builder needs non-hero slots.
* Mercenary hero placeholders.

## Done When

* Party slots can distinguish player, hero, mercenary, henchman, and empty slots.
* Hero selectors can render names and portraits.
* Guide notes can reference hero-specific behavior.

## Notes

Hero AI data may be partly manual. Keep factual hero cataloging separate from opinionated AI recommendations.
